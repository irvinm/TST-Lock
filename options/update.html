TST Lock Now Has Configuration Options!

With this update, you can now fully customize how locked tabs appear in Tree Style Tab—including custom icons, adjustable sizing, and visibility modes.

How to Access Options:

1. From the Add-on Toolbar Icon

Click the TST Lock icon in your browser toolbar or from Firefox's Extensions (puzzle piece) menu.

2. From about:addons

Open about:addons (press Ctrl+Shift+A), click TST Lock, and switch to the Options tab.

What You Can Customize:

  • 🔒
    Preset & Custom Indicators: Choose from popular presets, bundled classic lock icons, or type any single emoji or Unicode symbol (e.g. ⭐️, 🛡️, 📌).
  • 📐
    Adjustable Size: Scale the lock icon from 12px to 24px to match your preferred tab height.
  • 👁️
    Hover-Only Visibility: Choose between always displaying the lock icon or only showing it when hovering over locked tabs.
  • ☀️
    Live Tab Preview: Test your chosen indicator against simulated light and dark tab backgrounds before saving.
